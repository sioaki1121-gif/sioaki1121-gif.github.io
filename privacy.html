kujira clipper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Information we access

When you authorize kujira clipper with TikTok, the app receives an access token and a refresh token, and basic account information permitted by the user.info.basic scope. The app uses the video.upload scope only to upload video files to your TikTok drafts (inbox).

2. How information is used

Tokens are used only to upload videos selected by the user to the user's own TikTok account as drafts. No other actions are performed on the account.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored only on the user's own computer. The app has no server and does not collect, sell, or share any personal data with third parties.

4. Deleting your data

You can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions > Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
